# Bank of Russia ZSK high-risk check — technical probe

Date: 2026-09-17

Status: **PROBE COMPLETE / PRODUCTION MACHINE MODE BLOCKED**

## Official source and exact meaning

- Platform description: <https://www.cbr.ru/counteraction_m_ter/platform_zsk/>.
- Public check: <https://www.cbr.ru/counteraction_m_ter/platform_zsk/proverka-po-inn/>.
- Bank of Russia says the platform covers legal entities and individual entrepreneurs, not natural persons, and sends risk information to banks daily.
- The public check proves only the presence or absence of information that the Bank of Russia has assigned the queried entity to the **high-risk** group for suspicious transactions.
- It does not expose “green” versus “yellow”, the underlying criteria, bank-specific assessment, or whether restrictive measures were actually imposed. The Bank of Russia states its information is auxiliary for banks; restrictions require the high-risk assessment of both the regulator and the credit institution.

Product states, if a stable source becomes available, must therefore be:

- `high_risk_information_found`;
- `high_risk_information_not_found` after a successful dated check;
- `unavailable`.

Never label a negative public result as “low risk”, “green”, “reliable”, or absence of AML/KYC concerns.

## Browser and request workflow

The official page loaded in ordinary Chromium on 2026-09-17. Its form requires:

- INN;
- requester relationship (`Self`, counterparty, potential counterparty, unrelated, or other);
- a free-text request purpose;
- a browser fingerprint generated by FingerprintJS;
- Yandex SmartCaptcha token.

The form posts back to the same official page. SmartCaptcha is visible and mandatory in the public HTML. No separate documented public API, feed, API-key route, request limits, or machine-use contract was found on the reviewed Bank of Russia surfaces.

No form was submitted because doing so requires CAPTCHA completion. No CAPTCHA solving, automation, token reuse, fingerprint evasion, stealth, proxy rotation, or endpoint bypass was attempted.

## Freshness and cache policy

The Bank of Russia says platform information is transferred to credit institutions daily, but the public result page does not publish a historical interval or guarantee that a cached public response remains current.

If machine access is later formally provided:

- store the exact check timestamp and source URL;
- treat the result as a point-in-time observation;
- do not present it as current after an approved short TTL (provisional maximum: 24 hours, matching the stated daily cadence);
- retain historical evidence internally, but label it by observation date and never silently overwrite history;
- immediately back off on CAPTCHA, 403, 429, timeout, or schema changes.

The 24-hour value is a conservative product proposal, not a published CBR SLA, and requires confirmation before implementation.

## Production-mode decision

| Mode | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Documented API/feed | Not found | No public machine documentation discovered |
| Direct server POST | Prohibited/not viable | Mandatory SmartCaptcha and fingerprint; no bypass allowed |
| Headless/browser automation | Not approved | CAPTCHA is an intentional human challenge and production stability is insufficient |
| Operator-assisted manual check | Possible outside automated product | A human may use the official public page, but this does not satisfy an on-demand production check |

## Six Gates / acceptance

The Stage 1.5 **technical probe and production-mode decision are complete**. A production source adapter is intentionally not implemented. Found/not-found, PostgreSQL, and product-browser gates are not applicable until the Bank of Russia publishes an approved machine route or the product owner separately approves a lawful licensed source.
