# LEGAL LAUNCH GATE — Kontragent

Status: APPROVED MANDATORY PRE-SEO GATE
Date: 2026-09-17

Mass publication/indexing of the first 10,000 company pages is forbidden until the legal launch checklist is completed for the actual production scope.

This document is an internal launch checklist. Final legal positions, contracts and policies must be validated for the actual company, infrastructure and data flows before production launch.

## 1. Source-by-source rights

For every production/public source confirm and record:

- lawful access channel;
- automation rights/restrictions;
- storage/cache rights;
- permitted retention period;
- republishing rights;
- commercial-use rights;
- public SEO display rights;
- authenticated-user display rights;
- export/API rights;
- rate/load limits;
- expiry/purge duties;
- attribution/source-link requirements;
- contractual/licence conditions;
- personal-data restrictions.

A public web page alone is not proof of permission for bulk collection, indefinite storage or commercial republication.

## 2. Public vs private product projection

Classify fields and conclusions as at least:

- public/SEO allowed;
- authenticated-user only;
- on-demand only;
- consent/private only;
- internal-only;
- prohibited/excluded.

Person-related fields require stricter defaults. Do not publicly expose excessive identifiers such as passport data, SNILS, exact home address or other unnecessary sensitive identifiers.

## 3. Personal-data compliance

Before launch confirm the actual legal model for:

- collection and processing of personal data;
- purposes and minimisation;
- retention/deletion;
- correction/blocking/deletion requests;
- source/publication expiry;
- access controls;
- localisation/security obligations where applicable;
- processors/subprocessors;
- incident handling;
- consent flows for consent-only checks.

## 4. User-facing legal documents

Prepare and review as applicable:

- user agreement / terms of use;
- privacy policy;
- consent texts;
- cookie/analytics notice if relevant;
- rules for reports/exports;
- acceptable-use / API terms when those products launch;
- B2B contractual terms;
- support/SLA terms when promised.

## 5. Product claims and disclaimers

Risk, Summary, financial forecasts and future court predictions must be described accurately.

Required principles:

- data aggregation/analysis does not guarantee a counterparty’s future behaviour;
- forecast is not fact;
- court claim is not confirmed debt;
- unavailable source does not mean no risk;
- the service supports due diligence but does not replace the user’s business/legal decision;
- methodology limitations and data dates are visible where material.

Do not use marketing wording that is stronger than the evidence/methodology supports.

## 6. AI / automated conclusions

If AI-generated wording is used:

- factual inputs come from structured Evidence/Facts/Assessments;
- important conclusions are traceable to sources/calculations;
- model wording cannot silently create new facts;
- methodology/version is recorded for material reports;
- sensitive Person decisions require the separately approved legal/privacy model.

## 7. Company / commercial setup

Before paid launch confirm as applicable:

- legal entity/entrepreneurial structure;
- tax/payment model;
- invoicing/document flow;
- customer contracts;
- rights to third-party commercial data;
- contractor/IP assignment agreements;
- ownership of code/design/content;
- trademark/name clearance before final branding;
- accounting/support processes.

## 8. Government / regulated clients — later track

If the product later targets public procurement or regulated enterprise requirements, separately verify:

- Russian software registry requirements;
- infrastructure/localisation requirements;
- qualified electronic signature needs;
- formal information-security requirements;
- contractual SLA/support;
- procurement-specific documentation.

These are not required to block ordinary first B2B launch unless the target customer/contract requires them.

## 9. Legal gate acceptance

Gate passes only when the actual 10k SEO/public scope has a documented answer for:

- why each public field may be published;
- why each source may be processed in the intended way;
- what is excluded from public projection;
- how correction/deletion/expiry is handled;
- what user-facing legal documents apply;
- what disclaimers/methodology statements apply;
- who is responsible for ongoing source/legal review.

Any unresolved critical right to collect/store/publish blocks that source/field from the public 10k release, not necessarily the entire internal product.
