## Cross-market product standard inferred from public evidence

`INFERENCE`:

1. Registration identity and current status belong on the first screen.
2. Risk, positive evidence and data coverage are distinct concepts.
3. Courts need role, count, period and claim amount; a claim is not debt.
4. Bankruptcy needs legal stage and date, not a generic forecast label.
5. Enforcement needs current/historical distinction and published amounts where available.
6. The result must lead to a concrete next action and preserve source/date drill-down.
7. Unavailable information should limit the conclusion, not increase business severity.
